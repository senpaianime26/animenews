Thinking about getting into anime but don't know where to start? You're not alone.

## Step 1: Forget the stereotypes

Anime is not just one genre. It includes everything from deep psychological dramas to comedy, romance, sports, sci-fi, and pure action.

## Step 2: Start with accessible masterpieces

Here are some excellent entry points depending on what you already like:

**If you like action movies:**
- *Attack on Titan*
- *Demon Slayer*
- *Jujutsu Kaisen*

**If you like emotional stories:**
- *Your Lie in April*
- *Violet Evergarden*
- *A Silent Voice* (movie)

**If you like comedy:**
- *Spy x Family*
- *The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.*

**If you like mystery/thrillers:**
- *Death Note*
- *Monster*
- *Steins;Gate*

## Step 3: Use good platforms

Crunchyroll, Netflix, and HIDIVE are currently the best legal options for most people.

## Step 4: Join the community

Once you finish a few shows, talking about them is half the fun. Welcome to the club.
